Voicebot only answers after hearing it; everything you say is still written to its transcript. Empty means it always answers.

Thinking Sounds
Plays a random "hmm" or "uhh" in Voicebot's voice while it works out a reply, then a short pause
Thinking Sound Lines
One per line, made once in Voicebot's voice and cached; empty uses the built-in sixteen
Remake Sounds Every
Days before the thinking sounds are made again, so they stay fresh
Voice Print
Say "set up my voice print" to Voicebot, then talk for about ten seconds; after that it only answers your voice.
Screen History Command
Optional shell command; its output tells the agent what you were recently looking at
